About Our Institution

University has a rich legacy, being incorporated under the Sri Durga Malleswara Educational Society in 2007.
The institution continues to reflect the founders' strong determination and steadfast commitment to scaling the highest peaks and standards in education. The educational journey of the society began with the launching of NRI College of Pharmacy in 2007 and NRI Institute of Technology (NRIIT) in 2008 to enable aspiring students to obtain good education in and around Vijayawada.
